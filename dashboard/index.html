Local dates to plan around

  • Aug 18–20, 2026Virginia Tech move-in for first-year and transfer students
  • Aug 21–23, 2026Virginia Tech move-in for returning students. Radford's dorms also open around Aug 21 (unofficial date)
  • Aug 24, 2026Virginia Tech fall classes start
  • Oct–NovStudent complexes near VT pre-lease next fall (fall 2027 is already over 60% signed at some). This matters only if you want a student-style place
  • May 8–15, 2027Virginia Tech graduation week (main ceremony Fri, May 14). Graduates move out: more places open up, but trucks, movers and hotels are busy

The dates change every year. Check vt.edu and radford.edu for next year's.

Tips for an easier, cheaper move

  • Move on a weekday in the middle of the month (the 10th to the 20th). Weekend moves can cost 10–30% more, and the start and end of the month are busiest because most leases turn over then.
  • From May to September, book the movers or truck 6–8 weeks ahead. Around VT move-in week, book even earlier.
  • Watch for 10- and 11-month student leases (August to June) near campus. They're fine if you want one, but check the end date.
  • Try to make your lease end in winter. Then your next move or renewal also lands in the cheap season.
  • Ask about a move-in special from November to February. Landlords with empty units in winter are more willing to bargain: a free month, a lower deposit, or waived fees.
  • Plan a backup day in winter. Christiansburg gets about 20 inches of snow a year, and some complexes are on steep hills.

Income-based apartments

Christiansburg, Blacksburg & Radford · 2026 income limits

Three kinds of help

  • 🏡 Rent based on income (HUD Section 8 buildings, senior and disability housing). You pay about 30% of your income, whatever the apartment's normal rent is. These are usually the cheapest option, but waiting lists can be long.
  • 🧾 Tax-credit apartments (called LIHTC). Rent is capped and doesn't change with your income, but your household income must be under a limit, usually 60% of the area median.
  • 🎫 Housing Choice Voucher (Section 8 voucher). It pays part of the rent at a regular apartment whose landlord accepts vouchers. You pay about 30% of your income.

    Places near you

    Collected from affordable-housing directories, which are sometimes out of date. Call to confirm the program, rents and whether the waiting list is open.

    Tips

    • Apply to several places at once. Each property keeps its own waiting list, and being on one list doesn't stop you joining another.
    • When you call, ask: "Is your waiting list open? About how long is the wait? Do you take vouchers? What's the rent for a ___-bedroom?"
    • Have these ready: photo ID and Social Security cards for everyone, recent pay stubs or benefit letters, and bank statements.
    • Tax-credit rents aren't always low. The 2026 caps here are high (up to $1,346 for a 1-bedroom at 60%), so ask for the actual rent.
    • Students: a household where everyone is a full-time student usually can't rent a tax-credit apartment, though there are exceptions.
    • Keep your place on the list. Waiting lists usually drop you if they can't reach you, so tell them when your phone or address changes.
